### 📌 Overview

This project analyzes BMW vehicle sales data from 2010 to 2024 using Microsoft Excel.
The objective is to identify sales trends, top-performing models, regional performance, and key business metrics through an interactive dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

Analyze overall BMW sales performance

Identify top-selling BMW models

Track sales trends over time

Calculate key KPIs such as total sales, revenue, and average vehicle price

Present insights in a clean Excel dashboard

## 📈 Key Insights

BMW achieved over 25 million units sold across all markets.

The average vehicle price is approximately $75K, indicating strong premium positioning.

Sales show a consistent upward trend over time.

Models such as X5, 5 Series, and 3 Series are top contributors to total sales.

Revenue is highly concentrated in a few high-performing models.
